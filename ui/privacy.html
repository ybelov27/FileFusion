Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

Your files

File Fusion converts files entirely inside your web browser. The files you choose are never uploaded to File Fusion or to any other server, and we cannot see them.

Google sign-in

Signing in with Google is optional. If you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the page. This information is stored only in your browser's local storage on your device, to show your account and keep your conversion history separate. It is never sent to File Fusion servers (there are none), sold, or shared. Signing out removes it from your browser.

Conversion history

The converter keeps a list of recent conversions (file name, formats, size and time) in your browser's local storage. It never contains file contents. You can delete it at any time with "Clear history".

Third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts and the Google sign-in library from Google. These providers may receive standard request information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.
