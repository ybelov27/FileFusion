Frequently asked questions

Is File Fusion really free?

Yes. There are no limits, ads, or watermarks, and the source code is public on GitHub.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser on your own device. Your files are never sent to a server.

Do I need an account?

No. You can convert files right away. Signing in with Google is optional and keeps your conversion history separate per account on this device.

Which formats are supported?

Images (PNG, JPG, HEIC, WEBP, GIF, SVG, BMP, AVIF, ICO) to PNG, JPG, WEBP, PDF, BMP, ICO or a Base64 data URI; video (MP4, MOV, WEBM) to MP3, WAV or animated GIF; audio (MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, OPUS, FLAC, WAV) to MP3 or WAV; CSV, TSV, JSON, JSON Lines and XML to JSON, CSV, TSV, JSON Lines, Markdown, HTML, YAML, XML or SQL; Markdown to HTML, text or PDF; HTML to text, Markdown or PDF; and plain text to HTML, Markdown or PDF. There are also tools to compress and resize images, and to merge, split, reorder, rotate and compress PDFs.

Can I convert many files at once?

Yes. Drop in as many files as you like and use "Convert all" to process the whole batch.
